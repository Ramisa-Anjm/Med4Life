01 — OUR MISSION

Making healthcare
more accessible.

Med4Life is a youth-led initiative dedicated to supporting the Flemingdon Health Centre through fundraising and community engagement. The Centre provides essential healthcare, resources, and support to the Flemingdon community, including programs and services that make a meaningful difference in the lives of local youth.

We believe that as young people, we have a responsibility to give back to the organizations that invest in our community. Through Med4Life, we hope to raise funds, inspire others to get involved, and help strengthen the Centre's ability to continue supporting the people and youth it serves.

03 — OUR JOURNEY

Past Events

A look at what we've accomplished together.

November 2026

Grade 8 Open House

Grade 8 students who interested in attending MGCI came to the school for a visit. Our club welcomed these students and members of the community, sharing our clubs mission. Baked cakes were also sold to raise funds in support of the Flemingdon Health Centre.

November 2026

Fundraiser

We connected with the students at MGCI and sold fun keychains and stickers at our booth. Medical Trivia was also hosted! More than $100 of donations were made. All donations went directley to the Flemingdon Health Centre.

September 2026

Grade 9 Cirriculum Night

New Grade 9 students, families, and members of the community were welcomed at Curriculum Night. We connected with visitors and sharing what MED4LIFE is all about while selling freshly baked cakes to raise funds in support of our local health clinic.

May 2026

Junk Food Sale

Samosas were sold to the students at MGCI to raise funds for the Flemingdon Health Centre. A simple treat helped us turn student support into meaningful community impact.

September 2025

FHC Fun in the Sun

We joined the Flemingdon community in celebrating the Flemingdon Health Centre's 50th anniversary with a community BBQ featuring free food, prizes, a DJ, performances, and a health and information fair.

June 2025

STEM Night

Med4Life hosted a booth at MGCI’s STEM Night, where younger students participated in a mock surgery, enjoyed free chocolate, and learned about careers in healthcare and medicine.

May 2025

Fundraiser

Handmade crochet keychains were sold to the staff and students at Marc Garneau CI. Over $120 in donations were made! All funds were donated to the Flemingdon Health Centre.
